CUPRA Tribe

  • Product Design
  • End to End
  • Gamification

CUPRA Tribe is a loyalty app for CUPRA owners, built around challenges and rewards that connect everyday ownership with the brand, its events and community.

The project started with discovery workshops with the client, which shaped the app's information architecture, user flows and early wireframes. These evolved into high-fidelity designs that were tested through interviews with real owners, leading to further iterations and refinements before the final handoff to the client's product design team.

The main challenge was engagement: creating a reason to return to an app that would otherwise be used only occasionally. Challenges and rewards became the core mechanic, turning ownership into an ongoing digital experience.
