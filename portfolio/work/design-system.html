Diagonal is the design system behind SEAT and CUPRA’s digital products, spanning web, mobile, and a shared icon library across both brands.

Built from an established foundation, the system evolved into a scalable library of components, tokens, interaction patterns, and guidelines, documented across Figma and Frontify. Shared rules define how components behave together, ensuring consistency across products and touchpoints.

Designed to scale beyond web and mobile, with in-car infotainment as its next natural application.
